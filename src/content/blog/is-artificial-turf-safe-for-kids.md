## Decide what kind of play the area will support

A space for running or sitting has different needs from the area beneath a swing or climbing frame. Mark the equipment and its required clear spaces before selecting the turf. Follow the equipment and surfacing instructions, and use the applicable requirements for a home, daycare or shared playground.

Under play equipment, ask for the complete surface system’s impact-test documentation and how it relates to the equipment and planned installation. A soft sample cannot establish fall protection. Our [daycare planning article](/blog/artificial-turf-for-daycares/) covers questions for licensed programs.

## Make heat a layout decision

Turf can become hot in direct sun. Put shade and a cooler alternative into the plan, particularly where children sit or crawl. Check conditions before use and change the activity when needed; do not assume a short rinse makes the surface suitable for the rest of the day.

The [heat article](/blog/how-hot-does-artificial-turf-get/) explains the limits of product comparisons and temporary cooling. The same practical concern applies to the route from the door and nearby equipment, not just the largest turf area.

## Ask about the materials and care

Request documentation for the exact turf, backing, infill and any pad, including what was tested and the limits of the results. Evaluate those details alongside the intended use and care. A general “safe for kids” statement does not establish suitability for a particular play area.

Keep the surface clear of waste and debris and follow the product’s cleaning instructions. Include seams, edges and transitions in routine condition checks. Separate pet cleanup from children’s use when needed and keep cleaning products out of reach, following their label precautions.

## Before choosing a system

- Identify the ages, activities and equipment.
- Confirm equipment clear spaces and surfacing requirements.
- Obtain matching product and system test documents.
- Plan shade and a cooler place to play.
- Agree how cleaning and condition checks will be handled.
- Keep instructions and documents with the installation records.

Bring those needs to the [installation estimate](/estimate/). A useful proposal should explain the layout and selected materials without promising that any outdoor surface removes all risk.
