## Ask for the system from the ground up

Start with the existing ground and what will be removed. The installer should explain the finished levels, proposed base material and depth, and how the surface meets paths, beds and other features. Include any known wet spots or earlier site work in that discussion.

The base supports the finished surface and forms part of the drainage system. A product drainage rating alone does not show how the underlying ground or complete installation will handle rain. Ask where the water goes after it passes through the turf.

## Give each additional layer a purpose

If fabric is proposed, ask what it is intended to do and where it belongs in the system. Do not assume every yard needs the same barrier. For a play area, a pad and the turf above it must be considered together against the applicable use and equipment requirements.

The selected turf has its own backing and installation instructions. Infill, where used, must be matched to that product and purpose. A lawn, pet area and putting green can have different requirements; compare complete systems rather than collecting individual materials from unrelated examples.

## Keep rules and product requirements in scope

Confirm the requirements for the actual location and property use. The [Florida material guide](/guides/florida-turf-infill-rules/) covers its residential standard’s scope. The [installer guide](/guides/turf-installer-licensing-by-state/) helps identify relevant authorities and documentation.

A base minimum in a rebate program or another city’s guideline should not be treated as the design for your yard. Ask the installer to explain the proposed choice and identify any further assessment needed.

## Before approving the installation

- What comes out, and how will it be disposed of?
- What are the proposed layers, materials and depths?
- How will the base be prepared and the levels finished?
- Where will water go?
- Why is a fabric or pad included, if one is proposed?
- How are the seams, edges and transitions completed?
- What infill and care instructions apply?

Use the [quote checklist](/blog/how-to-read-a-turf-quote/) to keep those answers visible in the written price.
