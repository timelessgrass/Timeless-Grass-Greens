## Ask about the complete job

Include the area being converted, removal, excavation, finished levels, base and any proposed drains. Show changes to irrigation, retaining features or nearby hard surfaces. Identify whether the project is a private home, shared area or commercial site.

Ask which office reviews each part and what information it needs. A square-footage estimate or product sample alone may not describe enough of the work for a useful response.

## Local examples that change the next step

In [Atlantic Beach](https://coab.us/DocumentCenter/View/15920/Turf-Installation-Guidelines), the turf guidelines address product and base documentation and approval before the base is covered. Ask the City how the current requirements apply to the proposal and schedule the relevant review before installation reaches that stage.

For an unincorporated Horry County property, [County Stormwater](https://www.horrycountysc.gov/departments/stormwater/engineers/construction/storm-water-permitting/) describes staff review of exemption requests. Send the full scope instead of treating a small lawn as an automatic exemption.

If you are relying on Thornton’s turf rebate, [the program requirements](https://www.thorntonwater.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/2026-residential_artificial_turf_info__rules-1.pdf) include a permit step before installation. Program eligibility and other project requirements should both be confirmed. These examples are not rules for neighboring jurisdictions.

## Coverage, community rules and other work

Ask how the proposed turf assembly is classified for the property’s coverage or stormwater requirements. The [impervious-surface guide](/guides/is-artificial-turf-impervious/) explains what product and base information to bring.

Check association requirements separately. Also identify any permits or qualified trades needed for work on irrigation, electrical or other systems included in the project. One response should not be treated as approval for an item it did not address.

## Questions before work starts

- Which permits, reviews or inspections apply at this address?
- What plans and product details must be submitted?
- Must any work be inspected before it is covered?
- How is the turf counted for coverage and drainage?
- What applies to other work included in the scope?
- Who is responsible for each step?

Bring the written response to the [estimate visit](/estimate/) so requirements can be reflected in the proposed work and schedule.
