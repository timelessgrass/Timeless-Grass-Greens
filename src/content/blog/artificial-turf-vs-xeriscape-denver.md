## Draw the activities before choosing materials

Mark the route from the door, the places people sit or play and any putting or pet area. Then mark the beds and other parts you would prefer to plant. This makes the comparison about a usable yard rather than replacing every square foot with the same material.

A water-wise planting plan still needs suitable plants, establishment care, irrigation planning and maintenance. Turf removes the need to grow grass in the converted area but adds cleaning, brushing and eventual removal. Allow for that work in the decision.

## Compare the whole layout

Consider sun and shade, existing drainage, access and transitions between surfaces. Turf can become hot in direct sun; a planted area cannot be assumed to provide the same walking or play surface. Choose each part for the job it needs to do.

Keep existing ground or drainage reports available. The preparation for a particular lot should follow the site and design, not a general statement about Denver soil. Ask each proposal to describe what happens at the boundary between planting, paths and turf.

## Check incentives and property requirements early

A lawn-removal program may require a planted replacement and exclude artificial turf. Use the provider named on the water bill and ask whether the exact proposal qualifies before work begins. The [Colorado rebate guide](/guides/colorado-water-rebates-and-turf/) identifies the questions and program distinctions to check.

If an association governs the property, obtain its current requirements for the location being changed. The [Colorado HOA guide](/guides/colorado-hoa-turf-rules/) covers the distinction between a private backyard and other areas. Public project requirements also need their own check.

## A layout comparison checklist

- Which areas need a surface for a specific activity?
- Which areas could remain or become planted?
- What establishment and ongoing care does each option need?
- How will heat, drainage and transitions be handled?
- Is an incentive confirmed for this provider and design?
- What is included in each installation price?
