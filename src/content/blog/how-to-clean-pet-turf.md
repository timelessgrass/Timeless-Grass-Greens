## Build the routine around the area your dog uses

Pick up solid waste every day, dispose of it appropriately and wash your hands afterward. Keep a waste bag or scoop close to the door so this is an easy daily habit.

Rinse the spots your dog uses, following the turf manufacturer’s care instructions and any applicable water-use rules. Cover the favorite corners and the area beside the gate as well as the open lawn. Check that water can leave the area. If it keeps pooling, record the location and have the drainage assessed.

Use a compatible turf cleaner when a rinse does not remove the residue or odor. Follow its dilution, application, contact-time and rinsing directions. The right frequency depends on use and the selected product, so keep a simple note of what you tried and whether the result lasted.

## Use disinfectants only for their stated purpose

Cleaning and disinfecting are different tasks. If disinfection is needed, choose a product labeled for the intended use and compatible with the turf system. Check the EPA registration number, pre-clean the surface and keep it wet for the contact time specified on the label.

Follow all label precautions, including protective equipment, rinsing and when people or animals can return. Do not substitute a stronger mixture for the labeled directions or mix cleaning products. An odor-control claim alone does not establish that a cleaner disinfects the surface.

## When the smell comes back

Record which area smells, how soon it returns and what cleaning has already been done. Check whether waste or debris remains around edges and whether the rinse reached the full affected area. Include any standing water in the discussion with the installer or care provider.

Persistent odor can require assessment of the routine, infill and drainage. It does not, by itself, identify a failed base. Ask for an explanation of any proposed repair before agreeing to replacement. The [pet-odor article](/blog/why-does-artificial-turf-smell-like-dog-pee/) explains what information is useful.

## A care checklist to keep with the product details

- Pick up waste daily.
- Keep leaves and debris out of the pet area.
- Rinse the areas actually used, as the product instructions allow.
- Use a compatible cleaner at its labeled dilution and contact time.
- Follow the label’s return-to-use precautions.
- Record recurring odor or puddles so a site assessment can address them.

When planning [a new pet area](/services/pet-turf/), include a practical route for cleanup and access to water in the layout. The free installation visit is a chance to discuss those details alongside the turf options and written price.
