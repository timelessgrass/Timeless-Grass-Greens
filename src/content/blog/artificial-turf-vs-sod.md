## Begin with how you want to use the lawn

Mark the space for play, pets, sitting or putting, along with paths and planted areas you want to keep. Consider when it will be used and where shade falls. You do not need to choose one surface for every part of the yard.

Sod may fit a preference for living grass and a willingness to provide the water and care it needs. Turf may suit a dedicated putting green or another defined area where its surface and care requirements match the intended use. A dog run still needs waste pickup and cleaning whichever lawn decision you make.

## Compare the ongoing work

Sod needs care while it roots and then an appropriate mowing, watering and lawn-care routine. Confirm the grass variety, site suitability and establishment instructions with the supplier, including any current watering restrictions.

Turf needs debris removal, product-specified brushing and attention to seams, edges and infill. Pet use adds rinsing and cleaning. Include that water and time when making the comparison rather than treating turf as maintenance-free.

## Give heat and drainage their own decisions

Artificial turf can become much hotter than living grass in direct sun. Plan shade and a cooler alternative for contact-heavy use; the [heat article](/blog/how-hot-does-artificial-turf-get/) explains the practical limits. A quick rinse should not be treated as a full-day cooling plan.

Both proposals should address existing drainage and the preparation needed at your property. New turf does not establish that a wet area will drain correctly. Show the installer where water currently collects and keep any drainage changes visible in the scope.

## Compare complete prices

- The same measured area and agreed layout.
- Removal, preparation and disposal.
- The exact surface and any associated materials.
- Irrigation changes or drainage work.
- Establishment or handover instructions.
- Ongoing care and eventual renewal.

Confirm any rebate directly with the provider before including it in the budget. For an artificial lawn, a [free visit](/estimate/) provides a chance to discuss options, a custom layout and a written price.
