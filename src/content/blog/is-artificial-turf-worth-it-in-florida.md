## Identify the improvement you want

Be specific about the problem: a worn path, a pet area, a space for putting practice or a lawn routine you want to change. Decide how much area needs that function and what you want to keep planted.

Artificial turf removes mowing and irrigation for grass growth from that area. It still needs leaves and debris cleared, product-specified care and pet cleanup. Compare the actual routine you would take on with the work you do now.

## Plan for sun and rain

Check the area during the hours you expect to use it. Turf can become hot in direct sun, so include shade and a cooler alternative for children or pets. A short rinse should not be treated as a lasting cooling solution.

Show the installer where water currently collects and where downspouts or drains discharge. Regional rainfall or soil descriptions cannot establish the preparation for your yard. The [drainage article](/blog/does-rain-drain-through-artificial-turf/) explains why the product’s rating is only part of the question.

## Check the property and the complete price

Identify the property type and applicable public and association requirements before finalizing materials. The [Florida turf guide](/guides/florida-turf-infill-rules/) covers the state standard’s scope, while the [HOA guide](/guides/florida-friendly-landscaping-and-hoas/) addresses private community questions.

Compare removal, access, preparation, drainage, turf, edges and cleanup in the price. Include care and eventual disposal in longer-term budgeting. Do not count a rebate or an increase in property value unless it is supported for the actual proposal.

## A decision checklist

- What activity or recurring problem will the project address?
- How much turf is needed for that purpose?
- When will the surface be used, and where is the shade?
- What happens to water at this location?
- Are the material and property requirements clear?
- Does the total fit the installation and ongoing-care budget?

A [free estimate](/estimate/) can help compare a full lawn with a smaller pet or putting area, using a custom layout and written installation price.
