## Compare alternatives for the same purpose

Start with the job the area needs to do: a putting green, a dog run, a place to sit or a lawn for play. Consider whether living grass, planting beds, an appropriate hard surface or a combination could meet that need.

Turf changes the work involved. It does not need mowing or fertilizer for growth, but cleaning, debris removal and eventual replacement remain. Water may still be used for cleaning. Avoid assuming a water-saving figure from another property or a sports field applies to your household.

## Include the costs beyond installation

Converting the entire yard removes planting space and can create a hot surface in direct sun. A smaller turf footprint may leave room for shade trees, beds and other surfaces, subject to the site’s conditions.

Turf materials can shed particles during use and weathering. Include maintenance that limits displaced material, a plan for drainage and the eventual removal of the surface when comparing options. A sports field’s measured impact should not be treated as a figure for your backyard.

## Ask about drainage and removal now

Have the proposal explain what happens to rainwater and how loose infill will be kept out of drains and surrounding areas. Follow the product’s maintenance guidance and address displaced material instead of repeatedly washing or blowing it away.

Ask what would happen when the turf reaches the end of its useful life. A recycling claim should identify an actual receiving option for the product and condition involved. Keep removal and disposal in future budgeting; do not assume the original base or infill will automatically be reusable.

## A comparison checklist

- What activity requires this surface, and how much area is needed?
- Could grass, planting or a mixed layout meet the same need?
- What water and care will each option use here?
- How will the layout handle heat and drainage?
- What materials are being installed?
- Where could the surface and infill go when removed?

The [turf versus sod article](/blog/artificial-turf-vs-sod/) helps compare everyday use. A free installation visit can help define a focused turf layout and price, including the parts of the yard you intend to keep planted.
